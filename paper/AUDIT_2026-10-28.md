# Audit of all past experiments (2026-10-28): what is VOID, what is relabelled, what is redone

Ordered by the author on 2026-10-28: "go through all past experiments and data and see any that need to be marked VOID and redone". Standard applied: `research-protocol.md` s5 plus the standing rule "Defend every result" (fairness proof, three strongest attacks answered, own-goal check). Method: four independent read-only audits (curious-net P1-P99 with C1-C28; curious-net P100-P198 with C29-C57; neuroblock-research with emergent series 1-3; the vi/X-series and X6.5-X6.9 audited by the coordinating session), then the coordinator re-checked the sharpest claims in code and data before accepting them. About 70 numbers were recomputed from results files by the auditors, plus 8 by the coordinator: **no number was wrong**. Every finding below is about design, wording or registration, not arithmetic.

Records are never edited: this file, a CORRECTION block in `LOG.md`, and annotation blocks at the top of `LEDGER.md`, `PREDICTIONS.md` and `SYNTHESIS.md` carry the new statuses. Status words: **VOID-REDO** (do not cite; must be redone with the stated design change), **RELABEL** (the experiment stands but its status word or framing was wrong), **CAVEAT** (stands, with a stated limit), **KEEP**.

## 1. Registration evidence (applies to everything)
- Git commit order is the only trustworthy evidence that predictions preceded results. The "Registered" clock times in `PREDICTIONS.md` are unreliable (P56-P59 say 13:25; the registering commit is 11:10). For X0.6, X0.7, X0.2, X1.1, X1.2, X0.8, X0.10 and X6.6-X6.9, a registration commit precedes the results file (verified from `git log -S`).
- **No independent pre-run commit exists** for X0.1 and X0.1b (predictions and results first appear in the same commit), for P183-P185 (CTX11c) and for E9 (P198). They are CAVEAT on this ground alone; for P183-P185 it compounds with a threshold set after an exploratory look, so they are VOID-REDO (item V5).
- Seed blocks of the vi program (28 blocks, 68000-95000) do not overlap each other (checked by script). Series 3 of the emergent optimizer tuned AND scored on seeds 0-9 (see V7).
