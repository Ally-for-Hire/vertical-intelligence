# Publication package (one page)

**Goal:** tangible, honest scientific results that a faculty sponsor at the University of North Texas can evaluate.

**Check everything in 15 seconds:** `python publication/reproduce.py`  (re-verifies the solid claims; prints PASS/FAIL with the measured numbers).

**Read in this order**
1. `CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`: every claim with its status (verified / partial / untested / not novel), the evidence file, and the limits. Start here.
2. `PROPOSAL_UNT.md`: the pitch to a sponsor, with what is shown and what is not, the plan, the ask, and UNT routes found.
3. `PAPER_A_DRAFT.md`: the first manuscript (how culling changes accuracy), numbers real, gaps marked TODO.
4. `EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md`: the experiments still needed (with success/failure rules written before they run, compute and cost).
5. Evidence underneath: `../selfscore/THEORY.md`, `../selfscore/VARIETY_HYPOTHESIS.md` (your theory, pre-registered), `../car/RESEARCH_LOG.md`, `../selfscore/RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

**Status in one line:** the cull theory and the agent-vs-network benchmark are solid at toy scale; your variety-window theory and the 10,000-brain experiment are designed and instrumented but have no result yet; they are the next work.
