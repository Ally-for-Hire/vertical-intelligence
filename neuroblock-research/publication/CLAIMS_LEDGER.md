# Claims ledger (what is proven, what is not) — 2026-10-26

Status key: **VERIFIED** = measured, with seeds/numbers and the file that produced it. **PARTIAL** = true in a narrow tested regime; limits listed. **UNTESTED** = designed or started, no result. **NOT NOVEL** = correct but already known.
Rule for everything below: no claim is stronger than its evidence; "evidence" points to files that exist in this repo. All experiments are toy-scale (<= 24 small problems, networks of tens of nodes) unless stated.
