# Claims ledger

Every belief the user has stated, turned into a claim that can be confirmed or denied. Status vocabulary: **SUPPORTED** (tested, holds with the stated caveats), **PARTIAL**, **NOT SUPPORTED** (tested in the stated form; evidence against or no effect), **REFUTED** (the math or a clean test says no), **REFRAMED** (the literal form is not testable; a testable form is given), **UNTESTED**. Experiment ids refer to `experiments/` and `LOG.md`. "Lit" ties each claim to published work; items marked `[verified]` were checked against sources in this project; `[unverified]` come from memory and are queued for checking in `LITERATURE.md`.

**Reading note added in iteration 13 (review 2.1, 2.2, 6.1).** (1) Every 'X% of the oracle gain' below is a ratio to a HARD mask on the true direct parents. That mask is not an upper bound: the learned soft prior beats it in pure-interaction worlds (+0.090 vs +0.080, expS2) and connectome worlds (+0.108 vs +0.098, q19). Read the absolute gains (with their SEs), not the percentages. (2) The learned gate is a map from cheap early statistics to 'attend to this input' for a small-data network; it is NOT evidence of learning causal structure: ground-truth-label priors are worse than own-label priors (expM +0.101 vs +0.128) and the AUROC for true parents is 0.56-0.73. Under Adam a soft input gate acts as a learned per-input learning rate and initial scale (the Meta-SGD / ARD family). (3) C1-C8 belong to the legacy Boolean/count-table line; their statuses were not revised after the v2 evaluation flaws recorded in LOG.md and should be read as 'legacy, unreviewed'.
