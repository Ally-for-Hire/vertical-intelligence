# NeuroBlock car/steering line v1-v5 (2026-10-23)

## What was built
- v2 Boolean block graph (flip/receive/send/connect, `state' = bias XOR parity(inputs)`), v3 the same on a delayed 2D grid with a local
  reward-gated rule: v3 FAILED (held-out 0.07-0.16, ~"drive forward"; grew to 185 blocks / 3,000 wires).
- v4 "the poll": one neuron, every neuron votes yes/no/abstain each tick, tie = coin flip, neurons split on sensor bits where an
  action's effect differs. On the car only "forward" was ever decided (steering questions tied 67% of polls); cause unmeasured.
- v5 "cascade of trust" (`cascade.py`, toy problems): neuron i sees inputs + earlier neurons; opinion head and trust head;
  `P(on) = t*p + (1-t)*0.5`; REINFORCE; grows on reward plateau. 20 seeds, 20k ticks: beats a coin flip on every learnable
  problem (e.g. AND+noise 0.82/0.89 vs best constant 0.75), stays at chance on an unlearnable one (gt50 0.50) and near the ceiling
  on mult-of-4 (0.73-0.74 vs 0.75); does NOT beat "always no" on ends-in-5 (0.89 vs 0.90); XOR mostly unsolved (0.53/0.65).
  Defects: always grows to the cap, later neurons stay near birth trust (~0.28) and dilute the poll ('last' mode beats 'poll' 5 of 6).

## Update: cap and cost runs (2026-10-23)
- Unlimited cap (run b): worse everywhere (poll mirror 0.89 -> 0.83, last-neuron mode collapsed to chance); ~28 neurons, all newborns stuck at trust ~0.28 = coin-flip voters.
- Exponential cost (run c): a newborn is kept only if accuracy rises by more than `COST0 * 2^(k-2)` (COST0 = 2 SE of a window, ~0.04). Poll accuracy: mirror 0.97, AND+noise 0.935, ends-in-5 0.922 (beats always-no 0.899), mult4 0.741 (ceiling 0.751), gt50 0.499, XOR 0.655. Almost all trial neurons retire (~1.1-1.7 kept), so the gain is mostly removed dilution, not grown structure; XOR remains unsolved. Car not re-run. Details: `car/RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

## Update: v6 consensus brain with a Fable review loop (2026-10-23)
One persistent brain, no problem id; random neurons re-read each other until >=70% of the VOTERS agree, then a poll; growth/retirement from the brain's own reward history with exponential cost. Fable's review found a hidden problem id in the one-hot encoding, forced-voter neurons (no hidden units possible, XOR unlearnable) and a retire bug; the fix (trust = probability of voting, signed encoding) took XOR from 0.555 to 0.844 (10 seeds) and, with a 96k mixed stream, to FINAL mirror .989 / AND+noise .981 / ends-in-5 .982 / XOR .964 (30 seeds, 3.6 neurons). Caveats: strong forgetting during sequential training (mirror .97 -> .80), many experimenter constants remain, toy problems only, car not re-run. See `car/RESEARCH_LOG.md`, `car/consensus.py`.

## Update: v6 on the car, continued training, and the v7 predictive agent (2026-10-23)
- v6 on the car (reward = env.score only): 5/5 seeds reached held-out >= 0.5 three evals in a row (ticks 9k-75k, minutes). Continued training did NOT improve consistently: single evals swing 0.14-0.83, 3/5 seeds end lower than they began (seed 0 collapsed to 0.14).
- v7 replaces the poll with predicted P(success) per action (Table / random-net senses, best-first pooling, think until TAU, explore + spawn when unsure, optional learned calibrator). Toy problems (10 seeds): tables solve parity4/6, adder2, mux6 near-perfectly with ECE .001-.007; random nets win on moons/circles but fail on parity and are miscalibrated at TAU .9; mixed + calibrator is the best all-rounder. At TAU .7 learning stalls once 70% sure (majority5 .87-.90 vs .99-1.0 at TAU .9).
- v7 on the car: B at TAU .7 steady (4/4 seeds, ~.79 last-5); A TAU .9 seed 0 climbed .72 -> .88 with calibrated confidence; many A/C seeds never took off (always-forward-straight) or fell after a peak. Not yet consistent. Details and next steps: `car/RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

## Update: "purely better than an equivalent neural network" (2026-10-24)
Method E of the predictive agent (tables + per-action recursive-least-squares arms over random features + learned calibrator + exploration at the rate its own 'sure' decisions fail + faster sense spawning) was compared with tuned policy-gradient and value-based MLPs under identical bandit-feedback conditions (`car/compare.py`, 10 seeds, pre-declared criterion). Result: criterion MET on all 10 development problems and all 11 held-out problems (design frozen before the held-out run), e.g. addmod4 1.000 vs 0.669 (policy gradient), checker3 .966 vs .964, noisy moons .993 vs .993 in 480 vs 880 samples. On the car (success = beat this situation's usual outcome; episodic training) the agent with one setting reached smoothed best .896 / last-5 .883 vs the hindsight-best MLP .879 / .728 and the 16-run MLP average .667, reaching .5 in 12k samples on every seed. NOT met: run-time cost (agent 2-100x slower per decision) and memory (12k-197k state numbers vs 1.3-1.7k MLP parameters); rare collapses remain (car start-from-rest context). Verdict: better in sample efficiency, end accuracy, calibration and tuning robustness; not purely better. Lessons: a moving success target makes learners unstable (use per-context advantages); train episodically when tests start from rest; judge claims on held-out problems; measure inference cost. Full numbers: `car/RESEARCH_LOG.md`.

## Update: consistency (2026-10-24)
Why the car agent sometimes got worse (12-seed instrumented runs): not sense growth (zero births/retirements after warmup) but a calibrator whose step size never decayed (weights moved ~0.5 per 1,500 ticks), so near-tied actions flipped on noise and a flip of 'throttle at rest' stranded every car (2.8% of evaluations, always single-evaluation dips). Also found: under the per-context success definition the agent is 'unsure' 99.5% of the time because tau=0.9 is unattainable (best action succeeds ~65%). Fixes on 12 fresh seeds: decaying calibrator step (method F) cut dips from 16/648 to 1/648; a champion/challenger validation guard (promote only on a strictly better score on 16 never-trained validation tracks) gave 0/648 dips, 0 drops over 0.02, worst evaluation .82-.85, final .90, with test tracks never used for promotion. Limits: guarantee is exact only on validation tracks; plateau ~.90 vs .94 hand controller; tau still unattainable; guard costs ~0.4x extra simulation. Details: `car/RESEARCH_LOG.md`.
